How to Password-Protect Your Life (Without a Password Manager Headache)

Strong passwords are your first line of defense against hackers, but memorizing them all is impossible—here's how to secure everything without losing your mind.

Most people use the same weak password everywhere, thinking they'll remember it. Or they write passwords on sticky notes. Both are security disasters waiting to happen. The good news? You don't need to memorize 50+ unique passwords. You don't even need an expensive password manager if you're not ready for one yet. This guide shows you the realistic middle ground: creating passwords strong enough to actually protect you, storing them safely without breaking a sweat, and building habits that stick.
