50 Wedding Toast Openers and Closers You Can Steal Word for Word

Never freeze at the microphone again — 50 ready-to-use opening and closing lines for any wedding toast, so all you have to do is fill in the names and speak.

Giving a wedding toast is one of the most nerve-wracking things most people will ever do — and the hardest parts are always the first line and the last line. Once you are rolling in the middle, telling stories about the couple usually gets easier. But that opening line, when every eye in the room is on you, and that closing line, when you need to land the toast and raise your glass with confidence, are where most people freeze up.

This guide gives you 25 openers and 25 closers, organized by style — funny, heartfelt, classic, and role-specific (best man, maid of honor, parent) — so you can pick the one that fits your relationship with the couple and your own personality. Every line is written to be used exactly as-is, or lightly customized with names and details.

Here are a few examples to start:

Openers:
1. "For those who do not know me, I am [Name], and I have had the job of keeping [Groom/Bride] secrets safe for [X] years — do not worry, I am only telling the good ones tonight."
2. "I was told a wedding toast should be like a mini skirt — long enough to cover the important stuff, but short enough to keep it interesting."
3. "I have known [Name] since [how you met], and if you had told me back then that I would be standing here today giving a speech at their wedding, I would have laughed — and then asked to be paid in advance."
