How to Clean Foggy Headlights for Under $5

If you're tired of struggling to see the road at night due to foggy headlights, it's time to take action. Not only is it a safety hazard, but it can also lead to expensive repairs or even a failed inspection. With this guide, you'll learn how to clean your foggy headlights for under $5, saving you money and ensuring a safer drive.

To clean your foggy headlights for under $5, you'll need the following materials:
- 1 cup of white vinegar
- 1 tablespoon of baking soda
- 1 soft cloth or sponge
- 1 pair of gloves (optional)
- 1 bucket or container
- Water
- A drill with a polishing pad attachment (optional)

Here's a step-by-step guide to cleaning your foggy headlights:
1. Start by mixing 1 cup of white vinegar with 1 tablespoon of baking soda in a bucket or container. The mixture will start to fizz and bubble, which is a sign that it's working.
2. Dip a soft cloth or sponge into the mixture and apply it to the foggy headlight. Make sure to cover the entire surface, but avoid getting any of the mixture on the surrounding paint or trim.
3. Let the mixture sit on the headlight for about 10-15 minutes. This will allow it to penetrate and break down the foggy layer.
4. Use a soft cloth or sponge to wipe away the mixture and the foggy layer. You may need to use a bit of elbow grease to remove the stubborn spots.
5. Rinse the headlight with water to remove any remaining mixture and debris.
6. Use a clean, dry cloth to wipe down the headlight and remove any excess water.
7. If the headlight is still not clear, you can use a drill with a polishing pad attachment to buff out the surface. This will help to remove any remaining scratches or imperfections.
8. Finally, apply a headlight sealant or wax to protect the headlight and prevent future fogging.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Using harsh chemicals or abrasive materials that can damage the headlight or surrounding paint.
- Not rinsing the headlight thoroughly, which can leave residue and streaks.
- Not using a soft cloth or sponge, which can scratch the headlight surface.

Tips for best results:
- Make sure to clean your headlights in a well-ventilated area, as the mixture can emit strong fumes.
- Avoid cleaning your headlights in direct sunlight, as this can cause the mixture to dry out too quickly.
- If you have particularly stubborn fogging, you can repeat the process a few times until you achieve the desired results.
- Consider using a headlight restoration kit, which can provide more advanced cleaning and protection for your headlights.

By following these simple steps and tips, you can clean your foggy headlights for under $5 and enjoy a safer, clearer drive. Remember to be patient and gentle when cleaning your headlights, as they can be delicate and prone to damage.
