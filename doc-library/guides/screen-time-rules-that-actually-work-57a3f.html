Parenting Basics

Screen Time Rules That Actually Work

Stop the battles over devices—learn the proven framework that keeps kids engaged without endless scrolling or meltdowns when it's time to stop.

Every parent knows the screen time struggle. Your kid is glued to their tablet, you set a limit, and suddenly you're the villain. But here's the thing: screens aren't going anywhere, and neither are your kids. The real question isn't "how do I eliminate screens?" It's "how do I create rules they actually follow?"

The problem with most screen time strategies is they're built on willpower and punishment. "No screens before dinner." "Only one hour on weekends." These rules fail because they don't address why kids love screens in the first place—they're stimulating, rewarding, and way more interesting than folding laundry.

What works instead is a combination of clear boundaries, appealing alternatives, and honest conversations about why the rules exist. This guide gives you a step-by-step system that parents across different family situations have found actually sticks. You'll learn how to set limits that feel fair to your kids, what to do when they push back (spoiler: it gets easier), and how to model healthy tech habits yourself.
