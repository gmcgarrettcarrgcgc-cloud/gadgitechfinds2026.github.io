How to Edge Your Lawn Like a Pro

A crisp, defined lawn edge transforms your entire yard from ordinary to polished—learn the techniques, tools, and timing that separate amateur yards from magazine-quality landscapes.

Your lawn edge is the border where grass meets sidewalk, driveway, or garden bed. A sharp edge creates clean lines that make your whole yard look intentional and well-maintained. Most people edge occasionally but without understanding *why* or *how* to do it effectively. The difference between a sloppy edge and a professional one comes down to three things: the right tool, consistent technique, and knowing when to edge. This guide teaches you all three, plus the most common mistakes that people make—and how to avoid them. Once you master edging, you'll see results immediately every single time you mow.
