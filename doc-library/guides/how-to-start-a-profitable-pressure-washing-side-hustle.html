How to Start a Profitable Pressure Washing Side Hustle

Turn your weekends into $500+ by pressure washing driveways and decks - no special skills needed, equipment pays for itself in weeks.

Pressure washing is one of the fastest ways to start earning real money with minimal upfront cost and zero experience required. Here's why: homeowners desperately want clean driveways and patios but hate doing it themselves. You buy or rent a pressure washer, show up, spend 1-3 hours working, and walk away with $200-$500 cash. In this guide, you'll learn exactly how to get started, price your services, find customers, and avoid the mistakes that trap beginners in low-paying work.

The Real Numbers

A basic pressure washer costs $300-$800 to buy, or $40-$80 per day to rent. Most jobs pay $200-$400 for 2-4 hours of work. That means even at rental rates, you make your money back on your first 2-3 jobs. Once you own the equipment, profit margins jump to 80%+ because your only costs are fuel and occasional repairs.

Why This Works Right Now

More people are working from home and spending money on their yards. At the same time, most neighborhoods have zero local pressure washing options, so competition is light. You can literally start with one craigslist posting and book jobs within a week.

What You'll Learn

This guide walks you through: choosing the right equipment, pricing your services competitively, finding your first customers, staying safe, and handling common problems. By the end, you'll know exactly what to do on Day 1.
