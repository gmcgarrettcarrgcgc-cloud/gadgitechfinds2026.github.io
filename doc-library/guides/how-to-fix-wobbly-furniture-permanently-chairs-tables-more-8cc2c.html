How to Fix Wobbly Furniture Permanently (Chairs, Tables & More)

That annoying wobble is not a life sentence for your favorite chair or table — with basic tools and about an hour, you can lock it rock-solid for years, not just until next week.

Wobbly furniture almost always comes down to one of three culprits: a loosened glue joint, a stripped screw hole, or an uneven leg. The fix that actually lasts is never "tighten the screw and hope" — that is the band-aid that fails again in a month. Here is what you need before you start:

1. Wood glue (yellow carpenter glue, not super glue — it flexes slightly with the wood and holds far longer)
2. A rubber mallet or hammer with a cloth over the head
3. Clamps (furniture clamps or even large rubber bands/bungee cords in a pinch)
4. A screwdriver matching your furniture screws
5. Fine sandpaper (150-220 grit)
6. Toothpicks or wooden matchsticks (for stripped screw holes)
7. A flat, level surface like a countertop or tile floor to test on

Step 1: Find the real source of the wobble. Set the piece on a hard, level floor. Push down gently on each corner one at a time — the corner that moves is where the problem is. For chairs, grab the seat and gently twist; listen and feel for where the movement or creak is coming from. Do not skip this step — fixing the wrong joint wastes your glue and your time.
