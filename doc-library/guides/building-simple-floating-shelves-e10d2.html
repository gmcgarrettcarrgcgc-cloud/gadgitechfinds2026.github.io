Building Simple Floating Shelves

Turn any blank wall into sleek, sturdy storage in one afternoon — no visible brackets, no power-tool experience required, just a clean floating look that impresses everyone who sees it.

Floating shelves look like they defy gravity, but the trick is simple: a hidden wood or metal support hides inside a hollow shelf box and locks straight into your wall studs. Done right, a floating shelf can hold books, plants, or dishes for years without sagging, tilting, or pulling loose. In this guide, you will learn exactly what materials to buy (and what to skip), the one step that matters more than any other — finding your studs — how to build the hollow shelf box so it is strong instead of flimsy, and how to mount it so it truly stays put. Get this part wrong and even a great-looking shelf will droop within a month. Get it right, and it will outlast the house you hang it in.
