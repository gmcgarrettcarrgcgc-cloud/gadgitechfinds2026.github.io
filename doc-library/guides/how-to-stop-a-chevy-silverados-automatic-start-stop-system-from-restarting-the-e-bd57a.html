How to Stop a Chevy Silverado's Automatic Start-Stop System From Restarting the Engine at Every Light

Tired of your Silverado shutting off and jerking back on at every red light? Here's exactly how to turn that annoying Auto Stop/Start feature off - both the quick way and the more permanent way - without breaking anything or hurting your truck.

If your Chevy Silverado shuts its engine off every time you come to a stop, then restarts it the moment you lift your foot off the brake, that's not a glitch - it's a fuel-saving feature called Auto Stop/Start, and GM has installed it on most Silverados since the 2019 model year. It's designed to save a small amount of gas and reduce emissions during city driving, but a lot of owners find it jerky, noisy, or just plain annoying - especially when towing, idling in a drive-through, or waiting at long lights. The good news: GM built in an easy way to turn it off for each drive, and there are also ways to disable it permanently if you never want to deal with it again.
