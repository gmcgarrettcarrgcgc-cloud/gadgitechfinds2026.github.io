How to Cook Steak Perfectly Every Time

Are you tired of serving overcooked or underseasoned steak to your family and friends? Do you want to impress them with a perfectly cooked, restaurant-quality steak? With this guide, you'll learn the secrets to cooking steak to perfection every time, and become the ultimate grill master.

To cook steak perfectly every time, you'll need: a high-quality steak, cast-iron or stainless steel skillet, tongs or spatula, meat thermometer, salt and pepper, and your choice of seasonings.

Step-by-step guide:
1. Choose the right steak: At least 1-1.5 inches thick for even cooking. Ribeye and sirloin are great for grilling; filet mignon for pan-searing.
2. Bring the steak to room temperature: Remove from refrigerator 30-45 minutes before cooking.
3. Season the steak: Sprinkle with salt and pepper, add seasonings. Marinate 30 minutes to several hours if desired.
4. Heat the skillet or grill: High heat for skillet with a small amount of oil, or preheat grill to medium-high.
5. Sear the steak: 2-3 minutes per side until a crust forms. Don't press down with spatula.
6. Finish cooking: Reduce to medium-low and cook to desired doneness using a thermometer:
- 120-130F: rare
- 130-135F: medium-rare
- 135-140F: medium
- 140-145F: medium-well
- 145-150F: well-done
7. Let the steak rest: 5-10 minutes to allow juices to redistribute.
8. Slice and serve: Slice against the grain.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Overcooking the steak
- Underseasoning
- Not letting it rest
- Pressing down with spatula

Tips for best results:
- Use a high-quality, fresh and well-marbled steak
- Cook at the right temperature
- Don't overcrowd the skillet or grill
- Let the steak rest sufficiently
