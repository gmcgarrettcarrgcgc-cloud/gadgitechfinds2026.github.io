How to File Your Own Taxes for Free

Filing taxes can be a daunting task, but with the right guidance, you can save money and time by doing it yourself. Every year, millions of people overpay for tax preparation services, when in reality, they can file their own taxes for free. By following this guide, you'll learn how to take control of your tax filing process and keep your hard-earned money.

Filing your own taxes can seem like a complicated task, but with the right tools and guidance, it can be straightforward and cost-effective. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Gather necessary documents: Collect your W-2 form, any 1099 forms, receipts for deductions, Social Security number, and spouse's SSN if married.

2. Choose a filing status: The most common filing statuses are single, married filing jointly, married filing separately, head of household, and qualifying widow(er).

3. Select a tax filing software: Free options include TurboTax Free Edition, H&R Block Free Online, and TaxAct Free File.

4. Enter your income information: Enter your W-2 and 1099 forms and report any other income earned.

5. Claim deductions and credits: Common deductions include charitable donations, medical expenses, and mortgage interest. Credits include the EITC and Child Tax Credit.

6. Review and submit your return: Review carefully for math errors or missing information.

7. E-file your return: Submit electronically with the IRS and keep a copy for your records.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Not reporting all income
- Claiming ineligible deductions
- Not keeping accurate records
- Not e-filing your return

Tips for best results:
- Start early to avoid last-minute stress
- Use reputable tax filing software
- Keep accurate records of income and expenses
- Don't be afraid to ask for help
