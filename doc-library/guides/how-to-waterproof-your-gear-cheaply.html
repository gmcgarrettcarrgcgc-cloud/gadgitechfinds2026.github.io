How to Waterproof Your Gear Cheaply

If you're an outdoor enthusiast, you know how frustrating it can be to have your gear ruined by water. Whether you're hiking, camping, or just enjoying a day at the beach, water can quickly turn your adventure into a disaster. By learning how to waterproof your gear cheaply, you can protect your investment and enjoy the outdoors with confidence.

To waterproof your gear cheaply, you'll need a few simple materials and tools. Here's what you'll need to get started:
- A waterproofing spray or coating, such as Nikwax or Scotchgard
- A clean, dry cloth or sponge
- A well-ventilated workspace
- The gear you want to waterproof, such as a jacket, backpack, or shoes

Here are the step-by-step instructions:
1. Choose the right waterproofing product: Not all waterproofing products are created equal, so make sure to choose one that's specifically designed for the type of gear you're trying to protect. For example, if you're trying to waterproof a breathable membrane like Gore-Tex, you'll want to use a product that's designed for that type of fabric.
2. Prepare your gear: Before you can apply the waterproofing coating, you'll need to make sure your gear is clean and dry. This will help the coating adhere evenly and prevent any dirt or debris from getting trapped underneath. Use a soft-bristled brush or a clean cloth to remove any loose dirt or debris, and then let your gear air dry completely.
3. Apply the waterproofing coating: Once your gear is clean and dry, you can apply the waterproofing coating. Follow the instructions on the product label for the recommended application method, but generally, you'll want to spray or apply the coating evenly to the entire surface of the gear. Make sure to cover all areas, including seams and stitching.
4. Allow the coating to dry: After applying the waterproofing coating, allow it to dry completely according to the product instructions. This can take anywhere from a few minutes to several hours, depending on the product and the ambient temperature and humidity.
5. Test the waterproofing: Once the coating is dry, test the waterproofing by sprinkling a few drops of water onto the surface of the gear. If the water beads up and rolls off, the waterproofing is working. If the water soaks into the fabric, you may need to reapply the coating or try a different product.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Applying too much waterproofing coating, which can leave a sticky residue or damage the fabric
- Not allowing the coating to dry completely, which can reduce its effectiveness
- Using the wrong type of waterproofing product for the type of gear you're trying to protect
- Not testing the waterproofing after application, which can leave you unaware of any potential issues

Tips for best results:
- Always read and follow the instructions on the product label
- Apply the waterproofing coating in a well-ventilated area to prevent inhaling fumes
- Use a clean, dry cloth to apply the coating and to wipe away any excess
- Reapply the waterproofing coating periodically to maintain its effectiveness
- Consider using a waterproofing product that's specifically designed for the type of gear you're trying to protect, such as a product for leather or suede

By following these simple steps and tips, you can waterproof your gear cheaply and effectively, without breaking the bank. Remember to always choose the right waterproofing product for the type of gear you're trying to protect, and to follow the instructions carefully to ensure the best results.
