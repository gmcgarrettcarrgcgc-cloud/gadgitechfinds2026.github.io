Raising Backyard Chickens: Coop Plans, Breeds, and First 30 Days

Fresh eggs, zero grocery runs, and honestly the most entertaining pets you'll ever own. Here's exactly what to buy, build, and do in your first 30 days so no chicks die on your watch.

Chickens are the single best entry point into homesteading - cheap, low-maintenance, legal in most suburbs, and they pay you back in eggs within 5-6 months.

CHOOSING YOUR BREED (for beginners)
- Rhode Island Reds - hardy, great layers, handle cold well
- Buff Orpingtons - calm, great with kids
- Australorps - excellent layers, very docile
