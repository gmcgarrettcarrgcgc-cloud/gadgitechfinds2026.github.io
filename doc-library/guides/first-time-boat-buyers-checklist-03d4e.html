First-Time Boat Buyer's Checklist

Buying your first boat should feel like freedom, not a financial trap — this checklist walks you through exactly what to check, ask, and budget for before you sign anything.

So you're ready to buy your first boat. Exciting! But here's the truth: most first-time boat buyers overspend, get stuck with hidden problems, or buy a boat that doesn't fit how they actually plan to use it. This guide fixes that with a simple, step-by-step approach anyone can follow.

1. Figure Out Your "Why" Before You Shop
Before you look at a single listing, answer this: what will you actually do with the boat? Fishing on a calm lake is a completely different boat than pulling kids on tubes behind a speedboat, or cruising coastal waters. Write down your top 3 uses, how many people you'll usually have on board, and where you'll mostly use it (lake, river, or ocean). This one step prevents 90% of buyer's remorse.
