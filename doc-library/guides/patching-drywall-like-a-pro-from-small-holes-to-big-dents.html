Home Repair

Patching Drywall Like a Pro: From Small Holes to Big Dents

Forget expensive contractors - learn exactly how to fill, sand, and finish every drywall damage from nail holes to boot kicks in one afternoon. Your walls will look professionally repaired for less than a coffee.

Drywall damage is one of the easiest home repairs to tackle yourself, and the tools are cheap. Whether it's a pencil-sized hole from a picture frame or a fist-sized dent from moving furniture, the fix follows the same basic recipe: fill it, smooth it, paint it. Most people think they need special skills, but you really just need the right materials and patience.

This guide walks you through every common scenario - tiny nail holes, medium gouges, big dents, and corner damage. By the end, you'll know exactly which repair method to use, how to apply joint compound without it cracking, and how to sand it flush so the patch vanishes completely.
