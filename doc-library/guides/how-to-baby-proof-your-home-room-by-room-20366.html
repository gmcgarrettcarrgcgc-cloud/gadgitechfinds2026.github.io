How to Baby-Proof Your Home Room by Room

Create a safe, curious-friendly home where your baby can explore without constant supervision—this room-by-room guide shows you exactly what to secure, remove, and install to prevent common injuries.

Baby-proofing isn't about making your home prison-like or removing all risk—it's about removing *preventable* risks so your baby can play, explore, and develop safely while you breathe easier. Most injuries happen in predictable ways: falls from furniture, choking on small objects, poisoning from household chemicals, burns near heat sources, and drowning in seconds of quiet water.

This guide walks you through every room your baby can reach, showing you what matters most and what's genuinely optional. You'll learn the difference between a baby-proofed home (safe for 6-18 months) and a toddler-proofed one (safe for climbers and openers). You'll discover which safety products actually work and which ones create a false sense of security. By the end, you'll know exactly what to do in your living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, and nursery—without spending a fortune or driving yourself crazy.
