Pricing Your Product or Service the Right Way

Guessing your price is the fastest way to leave money on the table — or scare customers off completely. This guide gives you a simple, step-by-step method to price with confidence, even if you have never done it before.

Pricing feels like it should be simple: figure out what something costs you, add a little extra, and that is your price. But most new business owners get this wrong in one of two ways. Either they price too low because they are afraid of losing customers, and end up working hard for almost no profit. Or they guess too high with no real basis, and wonder why nobody is buying.

The good news is that pricing is not a mystery, and it is not about "gut feeling" either. It is a process — and once you learn it, you can use it for any product or service you ever sell.

Here is the first thing to understand: your price has to do two jobs at once. It has to cover everything it actually costs you to deliver what you are selling, AND it has to match what your specific customer is willing to pay for the value they are getting. Miss either one, and the price is wrong — even if it feels right.
