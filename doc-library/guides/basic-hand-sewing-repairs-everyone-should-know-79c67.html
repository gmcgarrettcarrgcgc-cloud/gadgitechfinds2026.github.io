Sewing & Mending

Basic Hand-Sewing Repairs Everyone Should Know

Never throw out a good shirt over a popped button or a tiny rip again — learn the five hand-sewing repairs that save your clothes (and your money) in under 15 minutes each.

You do not need a sewing machine, a class, or years of practice to fix most of the clothing damage that happens in everyday life. A popped button, a small tear, a fallen hem, or a split seam — these are the top reasons perfectly good clothes end up in the donation pile or the trash, and every single one of them can be fixed by hand in less time than it takes to watch an episode of your favorite show.

This guide walks you through the five most common hand-sewing repairs, step by step, using only a needle, thread, and a few basic tools you probably already have at home. No experience needed. No fancy stitches to memorize. Just clear, simple instructions written for someone who has genuinely never threaded a needle before.

Here is what you will learn: how to pick the right needle and thread, how to sew a button back on so it never falls off again, how to fix a small rip or hole so it is barely visible, how to repair a fallen hem so your pants and skirts stay the right length, and how to close a split seam before it gets worse.
