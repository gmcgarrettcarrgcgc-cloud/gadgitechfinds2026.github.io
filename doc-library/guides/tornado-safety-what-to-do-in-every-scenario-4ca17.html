Tornado Safety: What to Do in Every Scenario

Tornadoes can form in minutes with almost no warning — this guide gives you a clear, step-by-step plan for exactly what to do no matter where you are when one strikes.

Tornadoes are one of the fastest-forming, most unpredictable weather disasters there is. Some give you 20 minutes of warning. Others give you less than 5. The difference between staying safe and getting seriously hurt almost always comes down to one thing: knowing exactly what to do, right now, without having to think it through in the moment.

This guide breaks tornado safety down into simple, no-guesswork steps for every situation you might actually find yourself in — at home, in a car, at work, outdoors, or in a mobile home. You will learn the real difference between a "Watch" and a "Warning" (most people get this wrong), the safest spot in any building, and the biggest myths that could get you killed if you believe them (yes, including the overpass one).

By the end, you will have a clear action plan for your household, so when the sirens go off, you are not scrambling — you already know exactly where to go and what to grab.
