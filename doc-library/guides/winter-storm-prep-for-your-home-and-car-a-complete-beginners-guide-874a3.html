Winter Storm Prep for Your Home and Car: A Complete Beginner's Guide

A single winter storm can trap you at home for days or strand you on the road for hours — this guide gives you a simple, step-by-step checklist to protect your family, your house, and your car before the snow ever starts falling.

When a winter storm warning hits the news, most people scramble at the last minute — and by then, store shelves are empty and roads are already dangerous. The truth is, winter storm prep is not complicated. It just takes a little planning before the cold arrives.

Start with your home. The biggest risks in a winter storm are losing power, losing heat, and having frozen pipes burst. Here is how to get ahead of all three:

1. Insulate exposed pipes. Any pipe running through an unheated space (garage, crawl space, exterior wall) should be wrapped with foam pipe insulation, sold cheaply at any hardware store. This alone can prevent a multi-thousand-dollar burst-pipe repair.

2. Know where your main water shutoff valve is located, right now, before you need it in an emergency. If a pipe does burst, shutting off water fast is the difference between a small mess and a flooded house.

3. Stock a 3-day emergency kit for every person in your home: 1 gallon of water per person per day, non-perishable food that needs no cooking, a flashlight with extra batteries, a battery-powered or hand-crank radio, and a basic first aid kit.

4. Have a backup heat source plan — but know that space heaters and generators must NEVER be run indoors or in an attached garage due to carbon monoxide risk. Keep a battery-powered carbon monoxide detector as backup if you use any alternate heat source.
