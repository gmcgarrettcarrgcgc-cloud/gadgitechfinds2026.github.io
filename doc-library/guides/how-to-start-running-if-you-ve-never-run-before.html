How to Start Running if You've Never Run Before

If you've always wanted to start running but didn't know where to begin, this guide is perfect for you. Running is an excellent way to improve your physical and mental health, boost your mood, and increase your energy levels. By following this step-by-step guide, you'll be able to start running safely and effectively, even if you've never run before.

Starting to run can seem daunting, but with the right approach you can quickly become a confident runner. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. **Get Medical Clearance**: Consult your doctor before starting, especially if you have health concerns or chronic conditions.

2. **Invest in Proper Gear**: Running shoes (get gait analyzed at a specialty store), breathable clothing, water bottle or hydration pack, and a running watch or fitness tracker.

3. **Create a Running Plan**: Start with short, manageable runs and gradually increase:
- Week 1-2: Walk 30 minutes, three times a week
- Week 3-4: Run 1-2 minutes, walk 2-3 minutes, three times a week
- Week 5-6: Run 5-10 minutes, walk 2-3 minutes, three times a week
- Week 7 and beyond: Continue increasing running time and distance

4. **Find a Safe Running Route**: Well-lit, flat, free from traffic. Use online mapping tools or ask local running groups.

5. **Start with Walk-Run Intervals**: Begin with 1-2 minutes running, 2-3 minutes walking.

6. **Focus on Proper Form**: Head up, shoulders relaxed, chest open, hips neutral, feet striking midfoot or forefoot, arms bent at 90 degrees.

7. **Stay Hydrated and Fuelled**: Drink plenty of water before, during, and after runs. Eat complex carbohydrates and protein 1-3 hours before running.

8. **Listen to Your Body**: Rest if you experience pain or discomfort.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Starting too quickly
- Not staying hydrated
- Not taking rest days
- Not investing in proper gear

Tips for best results:
- Find a running buddy for motivation
- Mix up your route and terrain
- Reward yourself for milestones
- Be patient - progress takes time
