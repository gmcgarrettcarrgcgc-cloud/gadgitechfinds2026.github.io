How to Start Investing with $100

Are you tired of living paycheck to paycheck and wanting to build wealth, but don't know where to start? Investing can seem daunting, especially when you think you need a lot of money to get started. With just $100, you can begin your investment journey and start building wealth, and this guide will show you how.

Starting to invest with $100 is a great way to begin building wealth. Here's what you need and how to get started:

1. What you need: A computer or mobile device with internet access, a bank account, valid government-issued ID, and you must be at least 18 years old.

2. Choose an investment platform: Robinhood, Stash, and Acorns offer stocks, bonds, and ETFs with low or no fees.

3. Set up an account: Provide personal and financial information and fund with a bank transfer or debit card.

4. Choose your investments: Consider a mix of low-risk and higher-risk investments like bonds and stocks, or a robo-advisor for diversification.

5. Start small and be consistent: Consider investing $100 per month to build the habit and wealth over time.

6. Monitor and adjust: Keep an eye on your investments and make changes as your financial goals or risk tolerance change.

Common mistakes to avoid:
- Investing too much in a single stock or asset class
- Not diversifying your portfolio
- Not having a long-term perspective
- Not monitoring your portfolio regularly

Tips for best results:
- Start early and be consistent
- Educate yourself on investing and personal finance
- Avoid emotional decision-making
- Consider working with a financial advisor or robo-advisor
