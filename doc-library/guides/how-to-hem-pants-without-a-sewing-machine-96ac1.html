How to Hem Pants Without a Sewing Machine

Too short, too long, or just bought the perfect pants in the wrong length? Learn 3 no-sew methods (plus one easy hand-stitch backup) that give you a clean, professional-looking hem in under 30 minutes — no machine required.

Nobody should have to skip buying a great pair of pants just because they are 2 inches too long. And you definitely do not need to spend $15-20 at a tailor or buy a sewing machine you will use twice a year. This guide walks you through the exact same no-sew hemming techniques tailors do not want you to know about — using tools you probably already have at home, like an iron and some fabric glue.

Here is what you will learn:

1. How to measure and mark your new hem length so it is even on both legs (the number one mistake beginners make is skipping this step and ending up with uneven pants).

2. The fusible hem tape method — this is the closest thing to an invisible, machine-stitched hem you can do with just an iron. It is strong enough to last through dozens of washes.

3. The fabric glue method — perfect for a fast fix when you need pants hemmed in the next hour, like before a job interview or a wedding.

By the end of this guide, you will be able to hem any pair of pants — jeans, dress pants, or leggings — in about 20-30 minutes, with results that look like you paid someone else to do it.
