How to Choose a Sleeping Bag for Any Season

Cold nights ruin camping trips fast — here is exactly how to pick a sleeping bag that keeps you warm, packs light, and never leaves you shivering at 2 a.m.

Picking a sleeping bag feels simple until you are standing in a store facing fifty options with confusing numbers on the tags. Here is the truth: almost every sleeping bag decision comes down to just three things — temperature rating, insulation type, and shape. Get those three right and everything else (price, brand, color) is just preference.

First, temperature rating. Every sleeping bag has a number like "20F" or "0F" printed on it. This is NOT the temperature where you will be perfectly cozy — it is the lowest temperature where an average sleeper will not get hypothermia while still feeling cold. A good rule: take the rating on the tag and add 10-15F to estimate where you will actually feel comfortable. So a "20F bag" really feels comfortable around 30-35F. This one rule alone will save you from the single most common mistake beginners make: buying a bag rated for exactly the coldest night they expect, then freezing anyway.
