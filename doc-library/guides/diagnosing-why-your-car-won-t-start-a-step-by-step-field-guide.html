Diagnosing Why Your Car Won't Start: A Step-by-Step Field Guide

Your car won't turn over - is it the battery, the alternator, a bad starter, or something worse? This guide walks you through the exact tests mechanics use to pinpoint the problem in 15 minutes, using tools you probably already own.

When your car won't start, panic sets in. But before you call a tow truck and spend $500, take a breath. Nine times out of ten, it's one of three things: a dead battery, a bad starter, or a corroded battery connection. And you can diagnose which one in about 15 minutes with basic tools.

This guide walks you through the exact same diagnostic steps a professional mechanic uses, in plain language. By the end, you'll know exactly what's wrong - and whether you can fix it yourself or need professional help.

What you'll need: a screwdriver, a flashlight or headlamp, and maybe jumper cables. That's it. Let's start.
