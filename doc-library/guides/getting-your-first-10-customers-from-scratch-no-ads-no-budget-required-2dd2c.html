Small Business Basics

Getting Your First 10 Customers From Scratch (No Ads, No Budget Required)

Landing your very first customers feels like the hardest part of starting a business — but it does not have to cost a dime or take months. This guide gives you a simple, step-by-step plan to get your first 10 customers using nothing but time and the right approach.

Starting a business is exciting, but there is one question that stops almost everyone in their tracks: "Okay... but how do I actually get customers?" You do not have an audience yet. You do not have money for ads. You might not even have a website. That is completely normal, and it is not a dead end.

The good news is this: your first 10 customers do not come from big marketing campaigns. They come from a handful of simple, repeatable actions that almost every successful small business used when they were just starting out — including companies you already know today.

Here is the first step to get you moving right now.

Step 1: Make a List of Everyone Who Already Trusts You

Before you try to reach strangers, start with people who already know and like you. Grab a notebook or open a blank note on your phone and write down:

- Friends and family
- Former coworkers or classmates
- People from clubs, gyms, or groups you are part of
- Neighbors

This is your "warm list." These are the people most likely to become your very first customers, or point you toward someone who will. Aim for at least 20 to 30 names — this list is the seed for everything that follows.
