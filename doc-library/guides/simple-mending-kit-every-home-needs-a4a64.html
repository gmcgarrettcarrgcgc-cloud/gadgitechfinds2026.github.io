Sewing & Mending

Simple Mending Kit Every Home Needs

Never throw out a good shirt over a popped button again — build a $10 mending kit that fixes 90% of clothing mishaps in under 10 minutes.

Every home needs a mending kit — not a fancy sewing box, just a small pouch of the right basics so you can fix a loose button, a small tear, or a fallen hem the moment it happens instead of tossing the item in a "fix later" pile that never gets fixed.

Here is the truth: you do not need to know how to sew to use this kit. You need six things and about three basic stitches, and this guide will walk you through both.

1. WHAT GOES IN YOUR KIT

You only need these items, and all of them together cost less than $10-15 at any craft store or Walmart:

- A pack of hand-sewing needles (get "sharps," size 7-9 — the medium ones)
- One spool each of black, white, and navy all-purpose thread (these three colors match most clothes closely enough)
- A small pair of fabric scissors or embroidery scissors
- 6-8 spare buttons in different sizes (many new clothes come with a spare button sewn inside a seam — save those!)
- A seam ripper (a small tool with a curved blade, used to undo stitches without damaging fabric)
- Straight pins (a small cushion or magnetic pin holder keeps them from disappearing)
- A thimble (optional, but saves your fingertip when pushing needles through thick fabric)

Put it all in a small tin, pencil case, or zip pouch. Keep it somewhere you will actually use it — a hall closet or laundry room, not a drawer you forget about.
