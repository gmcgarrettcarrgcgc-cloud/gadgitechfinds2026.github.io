Build a DIY Backyard Fire Pit in One Weekend (Full Blueprint)

A real stone fire pit for under $150 and one Saturday afternoon - full materials list, cut layout, and the fire-safety details most tutorials skip.

This is a genuine, permanent backyard fire pit - not a metal ring - built from retaining wall blocks. No mortar required, no permit in most areas for a pit under 3 feet diameter (always check local code).

MATERIALS (for a 36" inside diameter pit)
- 40-44 retaining wall blocks
- Paver base gravel (~10 bags)
- A tamper
