How to Choose the Right Mower for Your Yard

Stop guessing at the hardware store — this guide shows you exactly which mower type fits your yard size, terrain, and budget so you never overpay or under-buy again.

Picking a mower feels overwhelming because there are so many types, sizes, and price points. But it really comes down to answering four simple questions about your yard. Once you know the answers, the right mower almost picks itself.

Step 1: Measure Your Yard Size
Before you look at a single mower, figure out roughly how big your lawn is. You don't need an exact number — just a general size category:
- Small yard (under 1/4 acre, about 10,890 square feet or less): a push mower is usually enough.
- Medium yard (1/4 to 1/2 acre): a self-propelled push mower or small riding mower works well.
- Large yard (1/2 acre or more): a riding mower or lawn tractor will save you serious time and energy.

If you don't know your lot size, check your property tax records online or use a free satellite measuring tool like Google Maps' measure distance feature.
