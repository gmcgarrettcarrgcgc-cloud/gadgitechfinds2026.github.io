How to Fix Squeaky Brakes on a Honda Civic (Cheap Fixes First)

That ear-piercing squeal every time you brake doesn't always mean an expensive trip to the shop - most of the time it's a $10 fix you can do yourself in under an hour.

If your Honda Civic squeals, squeaks, or chirps every time you tap the brakes, take a breath - this is one of the most common (and cheapest) car noises to fix. Before you assume the worst, know this: squeaky brakes almost never mean your brakes are broken. In most cases, it's one of a few simple, inexpensive causes: dust and dirt built up on the brake parts, dry hardware that needs a dab of grease, or a small built-in metal tab doing exactly what it's designed to do - warning you that your pads are getting low. In this guide, you'll learn how to tell the difference between "annoying but harmless" and "stop driving and fix this now," plus the exact cheap fixes mechanics try first before ever recommending new parts.
