Speeding Up a Slow Computer in 15 Minutes

Your computer is dragging. Before you panic or pay for repairs, try these 15-minute fixes that actually work. Most slow computers just need a little cleanup.

A slow computer is frustrating — but the good news is, you can often fix it yourself in less than 15 minutes without any technical skills or expensive tools.

The most common culprits are: too many programs running in the background, a full hard drive, dust buildup inside your computer, or browser clutter. The fixes are simple and free.

Here's what you'll do: First, restart your computer (this fixes more than you'd think). Then, check what programs are running at startup and disable the ones you don't need. Third, clean out your temporary files. Fourth, check your hard drive for space. If you have time, unplug it and blow out the dust.
