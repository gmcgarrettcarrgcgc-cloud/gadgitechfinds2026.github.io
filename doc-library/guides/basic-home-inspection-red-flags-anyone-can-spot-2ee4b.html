Renting & Real Estate Basics

Basic Home Inspection Red Flags Anyone Can Spot

You do not need a toolbox or a degree to catch the warning signs that could save you thousands — just 20 minutes and this checklist before you sign anything.

Whether you are buying your first home or touring a rental, walking through a property with a trained eye can save you from a nightmare later. Professional inspectors are essential, but you can catch a surprising number of red flags yourself during a walkthrough — before you even schedule a professional inspection. Spotting these early gives you leverage to negotiate, walk away, or simply go in with your eyes open.

Here is what to look for and why it matters.

1. Uneven or sloping floors. Walk slowly across every room. Set a marble or a small ball on the floor near a wall — if it consistently rolls one direction, the foundation may have settled or shifted. Small dips in old homes can be normal, but a noticeable slope (especially a new one) can point to structural issues underneath.

2. Cracks in walls and ceilings. Hairline cracks in drywall are usually harmless and come from normal settling. But watch for cracks wider than 1/4 inch, cracks that run diagonally from door or window corners, or cracks that appear in a "stair-step" pattern on brick or block walls — these can signal foundation movement and are worth flagging to a professional.
