Hunting Basics

Beginner's Guide to Deer Hunting: Gear and Strategy

Your first deer season does not have to mean guesswork and rookie mistakes — this guide walks you through exactly what to buy, what to skip, and how real hunters actually fill their tag.

Deer hunting looks simple from the outside — grab a gun or bow, sit in the woods, wait. In reality, most beginners waste their first season on the wrong gear, the wrong spot, or skipping a required step that gets them turned away at the check station. Here is the truth: you do not need thousands of dollars of equipment to hunt well. You need the right basics, done in the right order.

Step 1: Get Legal Before You Get Gear
Before you buy a single item, confirm three things with your state wildlife agency website: you need a hunting license, you likely need a completed hunter education course (required for first-time hunters in all 50 U.S. states, regardless of age, in most cases), and you need to know your local deer season dates and legal weapon types for that season (archery, muzzleloader, or modern firearm seasons often run at different times). Skipping this step is the single most common beginner mistake — it can mean a fine or losing your harvest.
