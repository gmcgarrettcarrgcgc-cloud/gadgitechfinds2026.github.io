How to Reset the Oil Change Reminder Light on a Toyota Corolla

That little wrench or oil can icon staring at you every time you start your car? You can clear it yourself in under 2 minutes - no dealer visit, no tools, no cost.

If you just changed your own oil (or had it done somewhere that didn't reset the light for you), that maintenance reminder icon on your Toyota Corolla's dashboard will keep glowing at you like it's judging your life choices. The good news: Toyota built a simple manual reset into every Corolla, and depending on your model year, it only takes a button press-and-hold or a quick trip through an on-screen menu. There are two main methods depending on whether your Corolla has an old-school analog trip odometer or a newer digital multi-information display between the gauges. Both are explained fully below, along with exactly how to tell which one you have, common mistakes that make people think the reset "didn't work" (it almost always did), and a safety note about why resetting the light is not the same thing as actually changing your oil.
