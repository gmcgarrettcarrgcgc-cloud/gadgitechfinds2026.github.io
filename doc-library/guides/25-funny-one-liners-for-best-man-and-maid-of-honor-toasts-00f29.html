25 Funny One-Liners for Best Man and Maid of Honor Toasts

Blank on what to say when it is your turn at the mic? These 25 tested one-liners will get real laughs — plus the simple formula for weaving them into a toast that actually lands.

Standing up in front of a room full of people to toast the bride and groom is nerve-wracking enough without worrying if your jokes will actually land. The good news: you do not need to be a comedian. You just need a few reliable, funny lines and a simple way to use them.

This guide gives you 25 one-liners that work for almost any best man or maid of honor toast, plus the exact 3-step method for turning a single joke into a toast people remember for the right reasons.

Here is a taste of what is inside:

1. "They say marriage is a partnership. So tonight, I want to publicly congratulate [Groom] on finally getting a business partner who actually shows up on time."
2. "I have known [Bride] for 10 years, and I can confirm: she has never once let [Groom] win an argument. Marriage counselors, take notes."
3. "Behind every great man is a woman rolling her eyes. Congratulations, [Groom], you found yours."

These work because they follow a pattern: set up a relatable truth about relationships, then land it on the couple specifically.
