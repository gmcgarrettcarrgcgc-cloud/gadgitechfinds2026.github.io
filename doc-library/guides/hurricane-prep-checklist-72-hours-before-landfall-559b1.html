Hurricane Prep Checklist: 72 Hours Before Landfall

Stop scrambling when the storm warning hits — this hour-by-hour countdown tells you exactly what to do 72 hours out, so you are calm, ready, and safe while everyone else is panic-buying water.

When a hurricane watch turns into a warning, the next 72 hours matter more than anything else you do all year. Most people wait too long, then hit empty shelves and gas lines with a storm bearing down. This guide gives you a simple, hour-by-hour countdown so you know exactly what to do and when — no guessing, no panic.

72 HOURS OUT: CONFIRM AND PREPARE
1. Check your local emergency management website or NOAA Weather Radio for the official forecast track and timing. Do not rely on social media rumors.
2. Fill your car gas tank completely. Gas station lines get long fast and pumps can lose power.
3. Withdraw some cash. ATMs and card readers may not work if power goes out.
4. Charge every phone, laptop, tablet, and portable battery pack to 100%.
5. Review your family evacuation zone. Search "[your county] evacuation zone map" to confirm if you are in a mandatory evacuation area.
