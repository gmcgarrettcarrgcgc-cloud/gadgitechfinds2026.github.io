Emergency Weather Prep

Hurricane Prep Checklist: 72 Hours Before Landfall

When a hurricane is bearing down, the next 72 hours are everything. This checklist walks you through every step - from stocking water to securing your home - so you're ready before the storm hits.

When a major hurricane is forecast to hit within 72 hours, most people run to the store and grab whatever's left. This guide gives you a smarter, calmer plan. We'll cover water, food, documents, your home exterior, your car, and your family communication plan - in priority order, so you make the most of every hour you have.

Step 1: Water First
Fill every container you have. FEMA recommends one gallon per person per day for at least three days - so a family of four needs 12+ gallons. Fill your bathtub using a WaterBOB or just straight up. Fill pots, pitchers, sealed bottles.
