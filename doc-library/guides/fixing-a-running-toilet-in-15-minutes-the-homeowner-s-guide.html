Fixing a Running Toilet in 15 Minutes: The Homeowner's Guide

A running toilet wastes thousands of gallons a year and jacks up your water bill - but the fix takes 15 minutes and costs under $20. Learn the exact steps to stop it yourself.

A running toilet is one of those home problems that feels mysterious until you understand what's actually happening inside the tank. Here's the good news: 90% of running toilets are caused by one of two simple issues, both fixable without calling a plumber.

This guide walks you through diagnosing which problem you have, then shows you the exact fix - step by step - so you can stop the running in 15 minutes. The parts cost $5-20, tools you probably already have, and you'll save hundreds in plumber fees and wasted water.

What causes a running toilet? The toilet refills the tank constantly because either:
1. The flapper (the rubber seal at the bottom) isn't sealing properly, so water keeps draining out
2. The fill valve is stuck or worn, so it won't shut off when the tank is full

Diagnosing which one it is takes 30 seconds. Fixing it takes 10 more minutes.
