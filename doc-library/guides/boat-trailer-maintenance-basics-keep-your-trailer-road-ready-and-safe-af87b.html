Boat Trailer Maintenance Basics: Keep Your Trailer Road-Ready and Safe

Your boat is only as safe as the trailer underneath it — learn the simple checks that stop breakdowns, blowouts, and boat-ramp disasters before they start.

Most boat trailer problems don't happen on the water — they happen on the highway, usually because of something small that got ignored. A wheel bearing that wasn't greased. A tire that sat in the sun for three years. A light that quietly stopped working months ago. The good news is that boat trailer maintenance doesn't require a mechanic's toolbox or years of experience. It just requires knowing what to check, how often, and what "bad" actually looks like. This guide walks you through the exact routine — tires, wheel bearings, lights, brakes, the frame, and the hitch — in plain language, with clear numbered steps you can follow the very next time your trailer is sitting in the driveway.
