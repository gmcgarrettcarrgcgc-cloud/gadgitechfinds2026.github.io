Parenting Basics

Surviving the First 30 Days With a Newborn

Your newborn is here—now what? Learn the essential routines, feeding strategies, and sanity-saving tips that turn those overwhelming first 30 days into manageable (even beautiful) ones.

The first month with a newborn is a blur of feedings, diaper changes, and sleep deprivation. But it doesn't have to feel chaotic. New parents who set up simple routines, understand their baby's basic needs, and get help from day one report feeling far more confident and less anxious.

This guide walks you through what to expect physically and emotionally, how to establish feeding rhythms (whether breast, bottle, or combination feeding), how to handle sleep deprivation without losing your mind, and when to call the doctor versus when to relax. You'll learn the top mistakes parents make in week one and how to avoid them—plus real strategies for keeping your relationship intact and asking for help without guilt.

The first 30 days set the tone. Get this right, and you'll move into month two with actual confidence instead of just survival mode.
