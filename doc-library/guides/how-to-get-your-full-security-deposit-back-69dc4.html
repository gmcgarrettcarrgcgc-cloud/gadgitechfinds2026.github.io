How to Get Your Full Security Deposit Back

Do not let your landlord keep money that is rightfully yours — this step-by-step guide shows you exactly how to protect every dollar of your deposit, from move-in day to move-out day.

Getting your full security deposit back is not luck — it is preparation. Most renters lose part of their deposit not because they were bad tenants, but because they did not document anything and did not know their rights. Here is how to set yourself up for success from day one.

1. Document Everything at Move-In

Before you unpack a single box, walk through the entire rental with your phone camera. Photograph or video every room, every wall, every appliance, every floor. Pay special attention to:
- Existing scratches, stains, or damage
- Cracks in walls or ceilings
- Condition of carpets and flooring
- Appliance functionality (turn on the stove, run the dishwasher, check the fridge)

Timestamp these photos (most phones do this automatically) and email them to yourself and your landlord the same day. This creates a paper trail proving what damage already existed before you moved in — so you can never be blamed for it later.

2. Get a Move-In Checklist Signed

Many states require landlords to provide a move-in/move-out condition checklist. If yours does not offer one, create your own using a simple template. Walk through it together with your landlord if possible, and get both signatures. If they will not do a walkthrough, send your own checklist and photos via email so there is a dated record.
