Parenting Basics

Basic First Aid for Parents of Young Kids

Know the moves that could save your child's life. A step-by-step guide to handle the scary moments calmly and correctly.

Every parent dreads the moment: your child falls hard, starts choking, or gets burned. You freeze. You panic. You second-guess yourself.

The truth is, most childhood emergencies aren't rare freak accidents—they're predictable situations that happen in thousands of homes every week. And the difference between a minor scare and a real problem often comes down to knowing exactly what to do in those first few minutes.

This guide walks you through the exact steps for the most common emergencies you'll actually face as a parent: choking, bleeding, falls, burns, fever, and allergic reactions. No jargon. No "call an ambulance and wait"—we show you what *you* do right now, before help arrives.

You'll learn:
- The safest way to clear a choking airway (it's not what most people think)
- How to stop dangerous bleeding without panicking
- When a fall needs the ER and when ice at home is enough
- The right way to treat burns so they heal properly
- Fever facts that actually calm you down instead of scaring you
- How to spot a serious allergic reaction and what to do immediately

This guide is built on guidelines from the American Academy of Pediatrics and American Red Cross. Print it, bookmark it, or read it now so you're ready when you need it.
